About HigherSpace Connect

HigherSpace Connect was built for one reason: Kenyan university students spend too much time hunting across scattered websites, WhatsApp groups and rumour mills for information that should be easy to find — HELB disbursement updates, KUCCPS placement windows, scholarship deadlines, internships and part-time jobs.

We bring that information together in one place, alongside a growing set of resources built specifically for campus life in Kenya — a deadlines tracker, verified student discounts, and a directory of support and emergency contacts.

What We Do

  • Aggregate and summarise HELB, KUCCPS, scholarship and job news
  • Track upcoming deadlines so you never miss a window
  • Give students a space to share and verify campus information
  • Offer an AI assistant for quick, student-relevant questions

What We Are Not

HigherSpace Connect is an independent, privately run platform. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of HELB, KUCCPS, the Ministry of Education, or any university. Always confirm critical information — loan status, placement, fee balances — on the relevant official portal before acting on it.
