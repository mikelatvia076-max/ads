Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HigherSpace Connect ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our website and tools.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide directly, such as messages sent via our contact form or member chat
  • Usage data such as pages visited, device type and general location, collected via analytics
  • Data you enter into our calculators (GPA, budget, timetable, etc.), which is processed in your browser and is not sent to our servers unless you explicitly save it to an account

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the site and its tools
  • To respond to enquiries sent through our contact channels
  • To display relevant advertising through our ad partners
  • To monitor and moderate the member chat for safety

3. Advertising and Cookies

We use third-party advertising partners (including Google AdSense and other ad networks) which may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us operate the site (hosting, analytics, advertising), under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you by contacting us at info@higherspaceconnect.co.ke.

7. Children's Privacy

HigherSpace Connect is intended for university-age students and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Visit our Contact page.
