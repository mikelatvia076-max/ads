Frequently Asked Questions

Is HigherSpace Connect an official HELB or KUCCPS website?

No. We are an independent, privately run information platform for Kenyan university students. Always confirm loan, placement or fee status on the official HELB and KUCCPS portals.

Is it free to use?

Yes. All articles, the Deadlines Tracker and the community chat are free. The site is supported by advertising.

How do I report outdated or wrong information?

Email us at info@higherspaceconnect.co.ke or use the Contact page with a link to the affected article.

Can I contribute campus news or rumours?

Yes, through the University Rumours section and member chat. Please keep submissions accurate and respectful — see our Terms of Service.

Do you have a mobile app?

HigherSpace Connect works as a installable web app (PWA) — open the site in your mobile browser and choose "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience.

How do I get in touch?

Visit our Contact page for email, live chat and Campus AI options.
